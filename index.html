Slide 05 — Customer Discovery (Channels — Key Pivot)
CHANNELS: OUR BIGGEST PIVOT
Biggest channel assumption directly contradicted by interviews → Pivot applies most strongly to: Awareness
Day 1 — Hypothesis (Not Validated)
  • Instagram Reels + TikTok short-form content
  • Campus ambassador social posts
  • Assumed: students discover platforms via algorithm-driven feeds
  • Scalable & low-cost to produce
  • → Not validated by interviews
→
Day 10 — Evidence (Adopted)
  • WhatsApp groups + peer referrals
  • Students rely on existing trusted communities
  • Direct recommendations carry far more weight than ads
  • Discovery is social & trust-dependent, not algorithm-driven
  • Network effects = built-in, not paid for ✓

Why It Changed

Interviews revealed students don't trust platforms discovered through ads. They adopt tools recommended by someone they know — a classmate, a group chat, a friend selling furniture.

Decision: PIVOT

Drop Instagram/TikTok outreach entirely for launch. Invest in campus ambassador network and WhatsApp seeding. Deprioritise paid/organic social content.

Slide 06 — MVP
MVP — SMOKE TEST LANDING PAGE

Type of MVP

Smoke Test Landing Page (Figma prototype) — the simplest artefact to test whether students would sign up for a verified campus marketplace before building the product.

Chosen over concierge/wizard-of-oz because we needed to test sign-up intent at scale quickly, with zero build time.

Assumption Being Tested

"If shown a landing page for a verified campus-only marketplace, at least 40% of students will express sign-up intent — confirming demand exists before we build."

Method

Showed landing page mockup to 20 students (Figma prototype) on campus. Question asked: "Would you sign up for this today?"

Slide 10 — A/B Test #3
A/B TEST #3 — PRICING COMMUNICATION
Version A — Control

Abstract percentage: "5% transaction fee"
Standard marketplace language. No context on what you get for it.

Version B — Variant

Value-anchored: "€1–2 for a verified, scam-free transaction"
Connects cost to a specific, tangible benefit.

Hypothesis (drafted April 19 — before execution)

"We believe Version B will increase willingness to pay because anchoring on concrete value (safety guarantee) reduces fee aversion vs. an abstract percentage."

🟡 ITERATE

Price communication matters as much as price level. Always anchor on value delivered. Re-test with premium seller tools framing in next sprint.
